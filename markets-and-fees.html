Liquidation in brief

A position is liquidated when its margin balance (initial margin plus unrealized PnL) falls to the maintenance margin requirement, measured against the mark price rather than the last traded price. For one isolated position, ignoring fees and funding, with leverage L and maintenance rate m, a long liquidates near entry × (1 − 1/L + m) and a short near entry × (1 + 1/L − m). The documentation uses m = 0.5% only as an illustration; the live rate is not published as a fixed number, so use the estimated liquidation price the app shows. Details: docs.bitnxs.com/liquidation.

Risk warning. Perpetual futures with leverage can lose all of your margin quickly, possibly at a price worse than the mark price and without prior notice.
