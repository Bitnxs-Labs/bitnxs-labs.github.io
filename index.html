Bitnxs: an AI-agent perpetual futures protocol, on-chain

Bitnxs is an on-chain protocol for USDC-margined perpetual futures on BTC, ETH and SOL. This small site is maintained by the Bitnxs team as a plain-language reference: what the protocol offers, the numbers that matter, how funding and fees work, how an AI agent or script can read public market data, and the risks. The app is at www.bitnxs.com and the full documentation is at docs.bitnxs.com.

At a glance

MarketsBTC-USDC, ETH-USDC and SOL-USDC perpetual futures.
Max leverage50x on BTC and ETH; 20x on SOL
Margin and settlementUSDC
FundingSettled every hour between longs and shorts
Base trading fees0.02% maker / 0.05% taker
DepositsUSDC on Arbitrum One
WithdrawalsReview of about 5 minutes; may be held or rejected
WalletsMetaMask or any EIP-1193 browser wallet
Demo modeNone; every order uses real funds
