How hourly funding works

A perpetual future has no expiry, so funding keeps its price near the underlying. On Bitnxs, funding is a payment exchanged between long and short holders and settles every hour, 24 times a day. A positive rate means longs pay shorts. According to the funding documentation, Bitnxs is not a party to those payments and does not receive them.

What is published and what is not

The rate is determined dynamically by the deviation between the mark price and the index price. No closed-form formula is published, so this site does not invent one. The current estimate is on the trading screen as "Estimated funding" and through the public marketSummary query, whose fundingRate field is a percent per hour (0.0100 means 0.0100% of mark-price notional for that hour).

Worked example (illustrative)

At the snapshot below, a long of 1 BTC at a mark of 60,435.59 USDC, if the hourly rate stayed at +0.0100%, would pay about 6.04 USDC to shorts for that hour (60,435.59 × 0.0001). The rate is re-set every hour and can change or turn negative.
