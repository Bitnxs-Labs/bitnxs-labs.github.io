Risk & restricted regions

Trading risk

Leveraged perpetual futures magnify losses as well as gains. A small move against a high-leverage position can liquidate your margin in full, possibly at a price worse than the mark price and without prior notice. If the insurance fund cannot cover a liquidation shortfall, auto-deleveraging may close opposing profitable positions. There is no promised return, no yield product and no guarantee of profit.

Infrastructure and custody-related risk

  • Deposits are USDC on Arbitrum One. While funds are in the deposit contract they are not in your wallet.
  • The deposit contract is upgradeable and upgrades are managed by Bitnxs Labs; an upgrade may change how it behaves. The contract address is not currently published in the documentation.
  • No independent security audit of the deposit contract or Bitnxs Chain has been published.
  • Withdrawals are executed after a review and delay window of about five minutes, during which a withdrawal may be held or rejected.
  • Oracle and mark prices depend on external sources that can fail, lag or be manipulated. Transactions are generally irreversible.
